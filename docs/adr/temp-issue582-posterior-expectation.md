# ADR-TEMP-582 — Opponent beliefs price by posterior expectation

Ledger values every candidate-conditioned opponent feature by its posterior probability and represents unknown posterior
mass separately. Observed facts remain full-strength; top-candidate selection and confidence gates would discard evidence
that the #559 Opponent Snapshot deliberately preserves and would create discontinuous training signals.

The current matched-Brief path and `posture_gamma` retire. Top probability and posterior margin may remain observable
features, but neither gates candidate claims; any deliberate risk preference must be an explicit Valuation Feature rather
than a hidden rewrite of opponent knowledge.
