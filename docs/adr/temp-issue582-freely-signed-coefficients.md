# ADR-TEMP-582 — Valuation coefficients are freely signed

Valuation Features describe neutral facts and their coefficients may be any finite real number. A general or deck-resolved
configuration may therefore strengthen, erase, or reverse a preference without changing feature extraction. Encoding
subjective sign rules in the schema would make strategy partly code-owned and contradict the wholly tunable surface.

Validation still enforces exact vocabulary, completeness, finiteness, and genuine mathematical domains for non-preference
data such as probabilities and counts. Evaluation gates, constrained parity fixtures, and tuning objectives detect harmful
but structurally valid preferences; schema validation does not pretend those preferences are malformed data.
