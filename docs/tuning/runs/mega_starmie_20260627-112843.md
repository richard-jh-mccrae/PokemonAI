`{"index": N, "type": 7}` (a bare hand index, no `area`), so `_option_card_id` returned `None` for
*every* Trainer/Pokémon/Supporter play — silently disabling **every** roles/tags/stat Hypothesis on

why Salvatore was never played. Fixed in `pilot.py` (`_option_pokemon` resolves a `PLAY` option
against the hand). Effect: seeds-satisfied jumped 14 → **24/30**.

`card_functions.json`): it searches a Pokémon *and* evolves it the turn the pre-evolution was played.
