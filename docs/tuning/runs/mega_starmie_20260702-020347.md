## Consideration — the second-Mega-vs-Cinderace promote (prize economy)

My first Mega Starmie ex is Knocked Out; on the Bench sit a **Cinderace** (1 prize) and a **second Mega
Starmie ex** (3 prizes, `megaEx`), both with Energy. Who to promote?

Often **Cinderace**, on **prize math**. Opp took 3 for the first Mega (needs 3 more). Lead with the
second Mega and it trades → opp takes 3 → **they win**. Lead with Cinderace: it chips (50 → **100** on
a Fire-weak Archaludon ex / Duraludon — verified weakness ×2, `docs/rules.md:113`), the opponent spends
a whole KO to take **1** prize (→ needs 2), and I promote the fresh, fully-built finisher next. Prize
denial + a favourable trade + (as the accelerator) it loads the finisher off the Bench.
